ABOUT THE BOOTCAMP

About UNFOLD 2026

Bridging the Campus-to-Market Gap

Engineering students create thousands of brilliant final-year projects that die post-graduation due to lack of commercialization guidance, legal structuring, and early capital.

The Bootcamp Solution

UNFOLD is a 48-hour intensive residential program designed to transform engineering prototypes into market-ready MVPs with business models, legal structures, and investor pitching readiness.
